Neighbourhoods is not a token wrapped around a community app. The framework first defines structural validity: what actions are possible, what states are valid, and how a community coordinates. NHT sits above that layer as a settlement medium where value needs to move.

This separation matters. Economic incentives should interact with valid processes; they should not define what validity is.

## The role of NHT

Communities may use NHT to reward activity, fund grammar development, settle inter-community transfers, support stewardship, route value through the Bazaar, or coordinate commercial exchange. But the runtime does not depend on token-weighted validation for everyday coordination state.

A grammar may say that a compost batch must be verified before approval. The runtime enforces that. NHT may then be distributed once the verified transition occurs.

## The Bazaar connection

The Neighbourhoods Bazaar is concept-stage, but its direction is stable: authors can list grammars; communities can license, commission, or crowd-pool the cost of grammars they need. NHT provides a natural settlement layer for that economy of reusable coordination patterns.

## Liquidity routing

Neighbourhoods Liquidity Nodes are in design as community-directed liquidity routing across community-held backing currencies. The aim is not to force one currency on every community, but to let value move between them through transparent routing where communities choose to participate.
